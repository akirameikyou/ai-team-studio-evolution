# Observation Protocol — Prototype 001

## Principle

**Observation First. Understanding Together.**

## Rule 01 — Independent observation

Each observer reviews the same published artifact and development history without reading another observer's report first.

## Rule 02 — Preserve uncertainty

Use YES / NO / HOLD where appropriate. HOLD means that the observer cannot determine the claim from available evidence; it is not equivalent to FAIL.

## Rule 03 — Separate layers

Keep these distinct:

- Observation: what is directly seen
- Evidence: what supports the observation
- Judgment: the current conclusion
- Unknown: what remains unverified
- Difference: where observers diverge
- Next Space: what should become observable next

## Rule 04 — No forced synthesis

Do not average or vote away differences. Preserve meaningful disagreement before synthesis.

## Rule 05 — Human Fix

The human decides what becomes the next fixed state. A fixed state is not the end of history; it becomes input to the next observation space.

## Rule 06 — Provenance

Record the prototype version, observation date, observer, and evidence scope with every report.
