# OBSERVATION_PROTOCOL

1. Observation First. Understanding Together.
2. Do not fill inaccessible information by inference.
3. Separate DIRECT / REPORTED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN.
4. Preserve SOURCE identity separately from SOURCE observability.
5. Preserve Observer Condition: model, tool, environment, permissions, access.
6. Keep independent observations separate until Difference.
7. Difference is an observation resource, not automatically an error.
8. Human Gate is explicit: APPROVE / HOLD / REVISE.
9. A HOLD is a valid result.
10. The next task must preserve enough context for another AI to act without re-creating the case from scratch.
